About GlossGhost

Engineered Performance For Real-World Detailing.

GlossGhost was built from enthusiast-led testing cycles, not category theory. After repeated maintenance sessions with overlapping products, we identified the core problem: too many steps, too much redundancy, and inconsistent outcomes.

Our solution is a focused, single-source system designed around measurable performance: controlled cleaning, high lubricity wipe behavior, predictable gloss gain, and durable maintenance protection across paint, trim, and glass.

The result is a repeatable workflow that reduces product switching, improves process speed, and delivers a consistent showroom-grade finish.
