Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Shipping

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Delivery windows vary by location and carrier conditions.

Delivery Issues

If an order is delayed or arrives damaged, contact support@glossghost.com with your order details for assistance.

Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days for eligible items in original condition. Contact support to initiate a return request.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method after return inspection and confirmation.
